Punch Embroidery

Punch Embroidery:
a textile art technique where loops of yarn or thread are pushed through fabric using a specialized hollow needle, creating a textured, three-dimensional design.

How to:

  1. Start by stretching and securing a piece of woven foundation fabric on a no-slip embroidery hoop. Make sure the fabric is stretched tight!
  2. Thread the punch needle tool with yarn.
  3. Punch through from the backside and use the needle to make stitches in the fabric. Each stitch should creates a loop of yarn.
  4. Remove the cloth from the frame. Trim the remaining yarn ends and straighten any stray loops.

Materials:
